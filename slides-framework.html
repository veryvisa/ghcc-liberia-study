Sample Deck Framework

A skeleton to show one possible structure for a case-competition presentation.
Every box is empty on purpose. The ideas, words, numbers and design must come from your team, on the day, in answer to the real prompt.

Use it to
  • see what a complete pitch usually covers
  • split the work: one person per few slides
  • check nothing important is missing
Don't
  • copy the layout or wording as-is
  • fill every slide if the prompt asks for something else
  • let the template decide your idea
Change freely
  • merge, drop or reorder slides
  • pick your own title, colours and pictures
  • fit the real time limit and judging criteria

Before you present: team checklist

  • ☐ Our slides answer the exact question in the prompt
  • ☐ Every slide title states a point
  • ☐ One idea per slide, big text, few words
  • ☐ Numbers come from the reading (with source)
  • ☐ Everyone has a part to say
  • ☐ We cut slides we don't need
  • ☐ Timed run-through, under the limit
  • ☐ Who answers which kind of question
  • ☐ Saved in two places + a PDF copy
  • ☐ Works without Wi-Fi / without a projector
  • ☐ Our own words, our own design, our own idea
  • ☐ Breathe. Have fun. Learn.
